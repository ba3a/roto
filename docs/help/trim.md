# Trim a video

## Purpose

This screen files a video as a clip. You choose the part of the video to use, and the action and facing it shows.

## Steps

1. Drag the two handles on the filmstrip to the first and last frame to use.
2. Press the filmstrip anywhere else to move the playhead. The preview shows that frame.
3. Choose the Action and the Facing.
4. Press Add clip. Roto extracts the frames.

## Controls

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Cancel | Leaves the screen and adds nothing. It is off while Roto extracts frames. |
| Action | Files the clip under one of the project's actions. It is fixed when you change the trim of a clip. |
| Facing | Files the clip under one of the project's facings. It is fixed when you change the trim of a clip. |
| Add clip | Saves the range as a new clip and extracts its frames. A clip already in this action and facing is replaced, and a warning says what that costs. |
| Save trim | Saves the new range for the clip you are changing. Work that depends on the old range is made again. |

## Gestures

| Gesture | What it does |
|---|---|
| Press near a handle, then drag | Moves that handle. The preview follows it. |
| Press anywhere else on the filmstrip | Moves the playhead there. |
| Ctrl and mouse wheel on the filmstrip | Zooms the filmstrip. |
| Mouse wheel on a zoomed filmstrip | Scrolls it. |

## Tips and problems

- Handles stop on frame starts. The range is at least one frame.
- Trim to one cycle of the motion plus a little more. A short clip is faster to work with.
- See [Set up a clip](clip-setup.md) for the next step.
