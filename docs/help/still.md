# Add a photo

## Purpose

This screen files a photo as a one-frame clip. Roto needs a second photo of the empty wall, taken from the same spot, to cut the figure out.

## Steps

1. Press Choose wall photo… and pick the photo of the empty wall.
2. Check that the two previews line up.
3. Choose the Action and the Facing.
4. Press Add still.

## Controls

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Cancel | Leaves the screen and adds nothing. It is off while Roto saves. |
| Action | Files the clip under one of the project's actions. |
| Facing | Files the clip under one of the project's facings. |
| Choose wall photo… | Picks the photo of the empty wall. Roto compares the photo with it to find the figure. |
| Add still | Saves the photo and the wall photo as a clip. It is off until you choose a wall photo. |

## Tips and problems

- Take both photos from a tripod with the same exposure. A moved camera leaves a ghost of the wall in the mask.
- You can change the wall photo later in [Set up a clip](clip-setup.md).
