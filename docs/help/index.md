# Roto help

Roto turns reference footage into a palette draft. You polish the draft in your editor. Roto then publishes the polished frames into your project. This help explains every screen and every control.

## Open the help

- Press **F1**, or choose **Help > Help pane (F1)**. The pane shows the page for the screen you are on. **Help > All help topics** opens this index.
- Rest the pointer on a control. A tooltip shows the first sentence of its row in the Controls table.
- Use the All pages button in the pane to come back to this page.

## Pages

- [Home](home.md): the project, the library, the profiles and their clips.
- [Overview](overview.md): every action and facing of a profile, and what to do next.
- [New project](wizard.md): the wizard that sets up a project from a preset.
- [Trim a video](trim.md): import a video and choose the part to use.
- [Add a photo](still.md): import a photo and its empty-wall photo.
- [Import models](model-import.md): bring finished animations in as reference.
- [Set up a clip](clip-setup.md): the clean plate, the mask, the key frames and the brush.
- [Draw a clip](draw.md): the scale, the zones and the draft.
- [Handoff and publish](handoff.md): render the handoffs, paint them in your editor and publish.
- [New variant](variant.md): publish a recoloured copy of a profile by swapping ramps.

## The usual order

1. Make a project with the [wizard](wizard.md), once.
2. Name a profile on the [home screen](home.md).
3. Import a [video](trim.md) or a [photo](still.md).
4. [Set up](clip-setup.md) the clip.
5. [Draw](draw.md) the clip.
6. [Hand off and publish](handoff.md).

For how to shoot footage that works well, read the filming guide in the repository. For what each pipeline step does, read the pipeline overview.
